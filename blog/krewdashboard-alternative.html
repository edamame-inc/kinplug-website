Looking for a krewDashboard alternative

krewDashboard, from Mescius, is the most established dashboard product in the Kintone ecosystem — pivot tables, slicers, drill-down, an Excel-like feel. Taking that as given, there are still a few reasons teams evaluate alternatives. Here they are, from published sources only.

First, krewDashboard is a good product

Pivot tables, bar, line, combo, area, pie, scatter, radar and gauge visualisations, slicers, cross-filters, timeline and drill-down, with an Excel-like feel that users consistently praise. Nobody looks for an alternative because it lacks features.

The reasons that do come up are narrower: the pricing model, where aggregation happens, and fiscal-year handling.

Difference 1 — whether price moves with your user count

krewDashboard Simple edition is published at from ¥12,000/month per domain (¥120,000/year). The word doing the work is "from": krew's terms of service state that the fee varies with your Kintone user count, and a change application is required when that number changes mid-contract.

So every time the organisation grows and Kintone seats are added, the plugin cost moves too. For an IT or finance team trying to fix a budget line, that matters more than the headline number. Kinplug Dashboard is a flat per-domain price with unlimited users — no applications, no true-ups when headcount changes.

Difference 2 — where your data is processed

This is the question security review always asks. Kinplug Dashboard runs every calculation in the browser, under the viewing user's own Kintone session. There is no external server holding records, which means the answer to "does our data leave Kintone?" is a single sentence.

It also means Kintone's own permissions are the access control. If someone cannot see a record in Kintone, it does not appear in a dashboard either.

Difference 3 — fiscal years and period matching

Japanese target-vs-actual reporting assumes a fiscal year starting in April. And the common real-world shape is a sales app storing a genuine date field while the targets app stores the period as a dropdown reading "FY2025" or "Q1".

Kinplug Dashboard derives year, quarter and month from the date and matches them to those dropdowns automatically, with the fiscal start month configurable and set to April by default.

Which to choose

If deep Excel-style pivot analysis is the centre of what you need, krewDashboard is the proven option. If instead your requirement is a price that does not move with headcount, no external server holding your records, or fiscal-period matching out of the box, Kinplug Dashboard is worth trialling. Both offer 30 days free and both can be installed in the same environment — the fastest way to decide is to run them side by side on your own data.

Common questions

Why is krewDashboard priced with a "from" figure?

The published ¥12,000/month per domain is a starting price. Under krew's terms of service the fee varies with your Kintone user count, and a change application is required when that number changes during a contract. Confirm exact figures with Mescius.

Does Kinplug Dashboard charge by user?

No. It is a flat per-domain price of ¥9,800 / $69 per month with unlimited Kintone users, and every currently available plugin is included at that price.

Can I trial both before deciding?

Yes. Both offer a 30-day free trial, and both can be installed in the same Kintone environment, so you can compare them side by side on your real data.

Does switching require changing our apps?

No. Kinplug Dashboard matches apps on a shared key without schema changes, and dashboard definitions are stored as Kintone records.
